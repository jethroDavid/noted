# Noted: a remembered Sunday at home

Accepted direction from the UI discovery conversation, 2026-10-03.

## Visual thesis

A school-free Sunday in a Philippine home in the early 2000s: illustrated rooms emerge from memory around dimensional, softly shaded household objects. Welcoming, unhurried, familiar, and personal.

## The home

Tiled floors, curtains, a crocheted TV cover, calendars, sunlight through window grills, and recurring chairs and table connect three scenes. Family members leave small, cute, positive traces for each other.

| Scene      | Time      | Main object                       | Gesture                                                  |
| ---------- | --------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Television | Morning   | A slightly modern early-2000s CRT | Vertical swipes change channels; reels autoplay silently |
| Fridge     | Afternoon | Notes and photos held by magnets  | Move notes and leave messages                            |
| Photo book | Night     | Family photographs                | Turn pages                                               |

Horizontal swipes change scenes. The central object appears first and its illustrated surroundings follow slightly later. Background motion is subtle, like recognizing a memory. No sound. The eventual home defaults to the TV. Authenticated navigation currently uses the existing homes flow; these three scene treatments are future work.

## Login

The [memory entrance](./memory-entrance.md) is the first implemented surface. A bounded painted sala fades into ivory paper behind a centered invitation, with readable handwritten lettering and a tactile Google button. The room appears only after its effects are ready. Successful sign-in pulls the visitor through pronounced liquid distortion before opening their homes.

The login contains no foreground television. Household objects belong to the future authenticated scenes. The room supplies atmosphere without covering the entire page; the UI stays sparse, personal, and easy to use on a phone.
