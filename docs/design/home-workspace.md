# Home workspace

The home route continues the shared Kalam handwriting, ivory paper, sage ink, and uneven paper controls. The existing home, board, membership, upload, archive, and realtime APIs remain the source of content.

- Television is the initial scene. A compact single-row header holds Your homes, the truncated home name, and Members. Each object fills the remaining viewport; small tool overlays preserve space for the scene.
- Scene tabs are replaced by a small caption, three position dots, and a swipe cue. Horizontal mouse, pen, and touch gestures wrap through morning TV, afternoon fridge, and night photobook. Native previous/next controls appear only on keyboard focus and support arrow keys.
- Scene entrances are scoped to the selected scene's mount. Post, presence, and media-query updates do not replay the page or navigation.
- Interactive controls and draggable fridge posts are excluded from scene gestures; vertical TV gestures stay inside the clip viewport. Album photos accept horizontal scene gestures while normal clicks still open the full-size image. Pointer capture begins only after clear horizontal intent.
- The television keeps a 4:3 outer silhouette on phones and desktops, sized against both viewport dimensions so the room remains visible. Its 3:2 glass, percentage insets and bezel placement, proportional screen rim, and rounded shell scale together; control hit targets remain at least 44px. Portrait videos fit inside the screen without changing the TV shape. Its sala has dedicated landscape and portrait illustrations in the login artwork's painted style; the login image itself is not reused. The phone composition keeps recognizable room details above the TV and sunlight on the tiles below it. Both use the shared lazily loaded React Three Fiber renderer with a wider, subtle liquid-memory mask and the source image's actual aspect ratio. Artwork stays hidden until the first transparent renderer frame, then reveals; changing the responsive composition waits for readiness again. Reduced motion and renderer failure use a still image. Redraws pause in hidden tabs and cap at 24fps on phones.
- Clips snap vertically inside the screen. A finished clip advances to the next ready clip, skipping unfinished uploads and wrapping to the beginning; a single ready clip repeats. Off-screen clips and hidden tabs pause playback. Playback first attempts sound on. A browser NotAllowedError retries muted without stopping the reel and shows Tap for sound. The always-visible lower-left screen control works in both normal and fullscreen views. An explicit mute stays muted across other TV interactions; a TV click or keyboard activation can unlock a blocked start. Quiet Web Audio receiver hiss/hum is created only inside a TV interaction, and follows clip playback. Receiver audio stops when playback pauses, the tab hides, or the scene unmounts. Stock clips without audio retain the receiver sound only. Tap the video to pause/play; a centered play hint appears only while paused. The three-dot menu contains Delete clip, pauses playback while open, closes with Escape or an outside click, and returns focus to its trigger.
- Fullscreen preserves the same playlist, playback, sound controls, menu, and VHS texture. Phones expand to a viewport overlay with a focus trap and Escape/Exit controls; desktops also request native fullscreen and fall back to the overlay if unavailable. Resize alignment keeps the selected clip in view, and scene switching is disabled inside fullscreen.
- Manual vertical navigation wraps the same finite list: scrolling down from 5/5 goes to 1/5, and up from 1/5 goes to 5/5. Wheel gestures arriving at an edge finish there; a fresh gesture wraps, with remaining momentum consumed. Touch swipes require clear vertical intent from the edge. Arrow/Page Up and Down also wrap at the boundaries. GSAP animates the wrap through a temporary adjacent slot in 420ms, then restores the original list position. Only the destination element moves; no duplicate clip players or growing list are created. Finished clips use the same navigation, with smooth scrolling between neighbors. Reduced motion jumps directly. Resize, tab hiding, and unmount settle and clean up any active wrap.
- VHS texture combines 12fps canvas grain, scan lines, softened red/cyan color separation, a slow tracking band, and a brief tuning texture on channel changes. It overlays the glass without intercepting controls or swipes. Reduced motion holds the grain and scan lines still and skips tuning/tracking movement.
- The fridge retains the existing kitchen and door artwork. Its surrounding illustration dissolves into paper; compact tools and presence overlay the scene edge. Post positions still use the same normalized 7:10 surface, sized to fit both viewport height and width. Notes scale down on narrow phones.
- The photobook shows four real archived photos per spread, with dates and compact page controls. The spread and photo grid fit the available height. Only a changed spread turns; background data updates preserve the current page. Existing full-size viewing and permanent-delete controls remain available.
- Shared controls use paper artwork. Note, photo, upload, and member dialogs inherit the palette. Dialogs render outside transformed scene containers, trap focus, close on Escape, restore the opener's focus, and fit the viewport.
- Reduced motion skips scene entrances, paper springs, page turns, and animated scan lines. The actual media can be paused with its playback control.

Object shading and memory masks live in `apps/web/src/styles/home-scene.css`; layout and controls stay in Tailwind. No additional artwork or motion dependency is required for this pass.
